1 · The stall: yours near, the counter far

The satchel at the table's edge lays the cloth over the table: the tabs, the hand and the edge stay. What you have lies near you, all of it, at a glance; the counter at the far side is the Orkmaster's. Press a thing of yours: the Orkmaster lays out what it trades for, each priced against what you have in the week of the AI tool that goes first; hover one and the potions show it hatched. Take trades: the new crosses the cloth to you, yours goes back as Yours before. A leader taken sits down at its tab. A lock says what is always yours. In the hand, a question card's Next time sends its way of answering to the stall.

2 · The heroes' faces

Four heroes a leader's race, each its race's elder head with its skin or helm, its horns, antlers or wings and one detail changed (`heads/make_heads.py`); the hood stays the tier's gear, the unicorn's helm stays a warm metal (grey is the plain knight's), no skin takes the action's green. The moods are laid over each from orkUI's own mood heads. Read at the sizes the window uses: 24 × 22 and 48 × 44.

What it changes

One look says the whole setup. The near side is the project as it is set: its heroes, its sizes, its potions, its rules, how each question is answered, how a conflict is met. No summary page.

A choice is a trade, never a loss. What you gave up waits on the counter as Yours before, and taking it back is the undo. The receipt in the hand says what changes From the next task.

A price is a difference. Each ware says what it adds or saves against yours, in the first tool's week, and the potions show it hatched before you take it. Money is said as money, in its own colour.

The counter is short. It lays out only the thing you pressed; with nothing pressed, the Orkmaster's advice and what the night offers from how you answered.

What it gathers. Settings' Start plans by themselves, the night, A result that holds lands in dev, Give me time to look, the Target, Push after merging, the cloud run; the parties' place (leaders, sizes); the potions' order. What stays in Settings: sign-ins, the Board in the repository, the rules for any AI tool, the phone, keeping the computer awake, updates, logs.

Open for the owner. Does the stall replace Settings' This project whole, or sit beside it? Is the first open the first visit to the stall? Does a hero keep its school's name as its name (§164), or have a name of its own beside it?

An experiment of 6 November 2026, not a goal and not a spec. Every colour, face and size is orkUI's token; the cloth, the lock, the answer cards' art, the satchel, the fire and the heads' variants are drawn here first and would go into orkUI after a yes. The prices are made up.
