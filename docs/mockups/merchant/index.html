1 · The inventory: the chairs say whose, the hand holds it, the table lays the ways

A chair is a craft: empty, its back shows its table's texture; taken, the hero's race says the craft (a race is a craft in a project), so a new hero changes the face, never the craft. A hero's card: the craft's texture at its top left (whose), the one silhouette of every hero at its top right (what card). The chairs stand in the route's order; the Orkmaster seats the crafts of the project's stage, the empty chair seats one sooner, and at Live the Orkmaster lays a card in the hand. The Orkmaster's hand holds four cards (When, How much, What leaves this computer, The Orkmaster's questions); the potions at the table's edge are pressed as they are, and the discard pile takes a card back to its default. The backpack at the chairs' end opens the inventory: the table's edge is stitched, the chairs stay. Pick a chair: the Orkmaster's is the whole project, a leader's its party; the table takes that chair's texture. Press the chair again and its heroes lie on the table: a hero brings its school, its flow, its size and its temper. The hand holds that chair's other settings; a card pressed lays its ways on the table, priced against yours and hatched on the potions. What you set yourself is marked yours, and stays when the hero changes. Close the backpack: the lanes come back, a new hero sits down at its chair.

2 · Whose table: a texture a chair, a stitch for the inventory

No colour: a pattern in the ink at a few percent on the table's surface, under the cards, so whose table it is reads at a glance on any day, not only in the inventory. The inventory is told by its edge, a stitch like the backpack's seam: the mode by the edge, whose by the texture, so the two never mix.

3 · The heroes' faces

Four heroes a leader's race, each its race's elder head with its skin or helm, its horns, antlers or wings and one detail changed (heads/make_heads.py); the hood stays the tier's gear, the unicorn's helm a warm metal, no skin the action's green; the moods laid over each from orkUI's own. At the window's sizes: 48 × 44 and 24 × 22.

What it changes

The window's own axis. A tab already says through whom; in the inventory a chair says whose settings. Nothing disappears, nothing new to learn: the chairs stay, the table and the hand change what they hold.

A hero is a set. The chair pressed lays its heroes with their stats: school, flow, size, how much it decides itself, how often it says no, the cost. The hand never repeats them.

Yours outlives the hero. A hero brings its own ways of answering its party's questions; what you set yourself is marked yours and stays when the hero changes. Your trust is yours (§52.6).

Whose by texture, the mode by the edge. Tech's grid, Design's dots, Product's contours, Release's stones, the Orkmaster's plain felt; the inventory's stitch. No new colour: colour stays a signal.

One way in. The backpack after the chairs, apart from Stop all's door. Settings' … keeps the plumbing (sign-ins, the phone, updates, logs) and ends with How the project is set: Inventory.

Open for the owner. Inventory is a game's word: it goes into TERMS with plain words beside it (how the project is set). Do the seats (Does, Checks) go into a leader's hand, or stay with the hero? Do the textures go into orkUI for every day, or only for the inventory?

An experiment of 6–7 November 2026, not a goal and not a spec. Every colour, face and size is orkUI's token; the heads' variants, the backpack, the textures, the stitch, the lock and the answer cards' art are drawn here first and would go into orkUI after a yes. The prices are made up.
