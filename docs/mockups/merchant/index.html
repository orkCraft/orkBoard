1 · The hand is what you have, the table what you could take

The satchel at the table's edge sends the leaders and their lanes away: the Orkmaster sits alone at the far side and the table is bare. The hand turns into what you have, three fans, each a third of the width at most: Who, When and how much, How you are asked. Press a card: its ways lie on the table, a row a part, each priced against yours and hatched on the potions; a leader lays its party's own parts under it. Take trades: the new flies into your hand, yours stays on the table as Yours before. Back to the table: the leaders come back, and a new one sits down at its tab.

2 · The heroes' faces

Four heroes a leader's race, each its race's elder head with its skin or helm, its horns, antlers or wings and one detail changed (heads/make_heads.py); the hood stays the tier's gear, the unicorn's helm a warm metal (grey is the plain knight's), no skin the action's green. The moods are laid over each from orkUI's own. At the window's sizes: 48 × 44 and 24 × 22, the corner of a fanned card.

What it changes

No new surface. The stall is a mode of the hand and the table: the hand holds what you have, the table lays what you could take, as a lifted card lays its answers today. Nothing scrolls sideways; the fans are a third each.

Three questions, three fans. Who: the leaders and an empty place. When and how much: how deep, the look, the starts, your hours, the cloud, the push, the day's cap, the gate. How you are asked: the questions no one party owns. A party's own (its size, its questions, its conflicts) lie under its leader on the table.

A card's corner says what it is. A fan shows each card's left edge only: the leader's head and its party, or the rule's sign and one word, as a playing card's index. The whole card rises on hover.

A choice is a trade, priced as a difference. Yours stays on the table as Yours before, and taking it back is the undo; each way says what it adds or saves in the first potion's week, hatched on the potions before you take it. Money is said as money.

The Orkmaster keeps it. Alone at the far side, its bubble says what it would take and why; its pick is starred on the table.

Open for the owner. Is the satchel the way in, or Settings' This project? Does a fan of eight read at a 31-pixel edge, or does When and how much split in two? Do a party's own parts stay under its leader, or get a fan of their own?
