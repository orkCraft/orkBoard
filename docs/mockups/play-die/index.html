1 · The die, seen from above

Bigger, and seen from above as a die on a table: its top face large and nearly square (its number clear), the front and right faces strips of two cells that only peek out, so it reads as a cube, not a box. 22 × 20 cells at 3 px (66 × 60), the potions' pixel; the card's greys, the edge round it, pips in the muted ink; Play under it. Hover: it wobbles once (three steps, 300 ms) and stays lifted, never shaking on. Playing, its pips and edge turn to the ink and glow.

2 · The story of a cold start

Its faces of fate: it rolls the whole start, the stage line under it, and lands on what came of it: a green tick (orkUI's success) when the page answered, a red cross (orkUI's danger) when the start failed. A die on the table lies askew: turned by the lean token in the hand, as a laid card is, and the other way where it lands. Pressed, the die is thrown from the hand onto the pane, which turns black (today's start screen), and rolls while the preview starts, the stage line under it (Starting npm run dev · 2 s). It lands, and in its place the project's logo comes up as today (six steps, two blinks, the name under it, the band sweeping while it still starts: the user's product, as a game shows its studio). When the page answers, the black fades and the frame comes up. The roll lasts about a second, then the logo holds the rest of the start; nothing waits for the show: if the preview is already running, the die only wobbles in the hand and the frame opens at once. If the start fails, the die tips over and today's failure (the reason, the command's last lines) takes its place. Under reduced motion: no throw, no roll; today's start screen.
