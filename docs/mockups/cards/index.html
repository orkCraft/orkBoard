Orkboard · the card’s anatomy · two variants

Variant A. The window’s one sign is the task’s type (feature, bug, screen, mechanic, balance, page, tune). The stage is a small sign beside its word under the window (a page for planning, a sword for doing, a shield for checking). In the hand the window shows what the card asks (an alert for a failure, a target for a question, a check for done) and the label has the goal’s type as the small sign and the party’s name.

Variant B. The window shows the stage’s sign (a page, a sword, a shield; in the hand what the card asks) with the member’s or leader’s head small at its corner, like a badge. The label is the stage’s word, or the party’s name.

1 · The Board

Wide · 1280 × 800

Every card in the anatomy of Slay the Spire’s: the cost’s gem top left (only where the card spends), the banner with the card’s name, the goal’s element top right, one quiet sign in a framed window, the label under it and, at the foot, the goal it serves or what the card says. No head on any face. The Board has tasks in every stage for two parties with a failed check among them; the hand has the deck with the + card, three stacks and the discard.

2 · A card on the table, lifted

Wide · 1280 × 800

A task card pressed is lifted where it lies and turned over: the member’s head, its files, What it made and Open. The head is on the turned card only.

3 · Choose a card: words

Wide · 1280 × 800

A question lifted from the hand deals its choices as cards in a row over the pane, each in the same anatomy: its banner the choice’s words, its window the question’s sign, its foot the why for the picked one, its gem where that choice costs more. The picked one is raised and bracketed; Other… is a blank card with a +; under the row, one quiet button; the question stands over the row as a banner. orkUI has no question sign, so the window shows the target sign.

4 · Choose a card: pictures

Wide · 1280 × 800

When the choices are visual the preview fills each card’s window in place of the sign. The previews are simple drawings in orkUI’s tokens.

5 · Phone upright: Board and hand

Narrow · 390 × 844

The Board’s tokens and the hand’s cards in the anatomy, smaller: 40 × 56 tokens, 72 × 100 cards. The foot has no room and is on the lifted card.

6 · Phone upright: choose, words

Narrow · 390 × 844

The same choice at the phone’s width: four small cards in a row, the picked one raised; its why stands under the row, because a small card has no foot.

7 · Phone upright: choose, pictures

Narrow · 390 × 844

The pictures as small previews in the cards’ windows.
