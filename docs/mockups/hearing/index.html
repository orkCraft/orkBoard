1 · One card through a goal's life

The + card is a card's back. Pressed, it turns over into the card of the text (the field has the focus from the press itself, so a phone's keyboard rises). Sent, it turns back with its rune lit and goes face down to the Orkmaster. While the leaders talk it over, its rune glows, and their heads under it light up as each has spoken. Several goals at the Orkmaster are told apart by their rune and short name, and none is green. Hovered, a back only lifts at a corner. Pressed, it turns. When everyone agrees, the seal's own runes are the wait. When the wait ends, the card turns face up and flies to its lane.

2 · A card handed from where it was played, and played back there

Today a card leaves a leader and something appears in the hand, and the two never touch. Proposed: the head of the one who plays it gives one beat. The card then flies from the goal's card to its place in the hand, face up, with the asker's head already in its window, and says From <Leader>. Answered, it flies back into the goal. Dropped, it crumbles where it lay. With the goal out of view, it comes from the leader's tab. On the phone it comes from the crown. Never for what came while the page was hidden, never while a finger is down, at most three at once, and nothing moves under reduced motion.

3 · The hearing: thrown to the side you take

When a leader says no or doubts, the face-down card turns up in the middle, with at most three sides around it. Do it is on the right. Drop it or After … is on the left. The smaller one is at the top. Each side shows its leaders' heads and their own words. The middle takes your own words. Drag or swipe the card past a third of the way, press a side (one tap stays), or use the keys → ← ↑. Short of a third, the card comes back, and down does nothing. The toast has Undo, and a drop is sent only when the toast goes.

4 · The sketch: touched on the phone before the spec

A goal that is seen or heard gets two or three throwaway variants from its craft's leader, before its spec. Here it is the Dryad's: each variant is one self-contained page, kept beside the goal and never in the project's code. It is served in a sandbox (sandbox="allow-scripts" with no same origin, and a CSP with no network), so it reaches neither the Board nor the internet. On the phone you see one variant at a time, at the screen's width, and press it as you would the real thing. A · B · C are buttons, never a swipe, because the frame takes the gestures. The variants below are live: press them.

5 · The morning, the night, the evening

Morning: the first card after the night says what the night did, and Play it opens Play on the screen each goal changed. Night: with Work at night on, what would start by itself waits for the night's window. Build it now still starts it by day, from the phone too. Evening: each leader's look says in a line whether what changed went toward its aims, and what broke.

6 · The die: the play arrow on top

The owner: by default the die's top face could show play as an arrow; it is clearer and suits the die. Today the top face at rest shows the five, which says nothing. Proposed: at rest it shows the play arrow, drawn in the die's own pips (2 × 2 cells, the muted ink, the ink while it plays). The cross (the last start failed) and the spark (dev is new) stay as they are, because they say more than the arrow. Asleep (no preview: nothing to play) it stays the five, so the arrow never promises a Play that cannot start. The other faces keep the die's numbers, so it still reads as a die when it rolls.
