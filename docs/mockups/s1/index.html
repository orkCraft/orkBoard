Orkboard · the window as decided · mockup s1

This slice builds the frame: the tabs, the Board, Tech’s System, one thing open over the pane, the hand with the blank card, the deck, the stacks, Release’s card and the discard. The question card with its pick, and the route card, come with the next slice (drawn then).

1 · The Orkmaster’s tab

Wide · 1280 × 800

The Orkmaster’s tab. The tabs take their columns’ widths while the Board is shown, and the Board has no header of its own. The Orkmaster’s column holds only its own work; the other columns hold tasks at work as 128 × 180 cards (the member’s head turns; drawn still) and what is promised as dashed ghosts. The hand has one size of card: the blank card as a slim field, the deck and the discard as thin piles at the two ends, two stacks (a failure, then a question) and the discard. The question stack’s top card is lifted over the pane’s foot, the lane it is about raised a little: what a card asks is on its lifted state only.

2 · Tech’s tab, System

Wide · 1280 × 800

Tech’s header, the System map (containers, their named links, one container opened in layers) and one part lifted and turned over. The blank card is addressed to Tech, the map is in picking, and one part is pinned to the card.

3 · One thing opened over the pane

Wide · 1280 × 800

Open on a lifted card’s back opens the file over the map; ← goes back to the map as it was. The hand stays under it.

4 · The deck opened

Wide · 1280 × 800

The deck pressed opens a list over the pane, in the Orkmaster’s order, with a search. A goal not started has a drag handle; one waits under the moon with its hour.

5 · The discard opened

Wide · 1280 × 800

The discard pressed opens over the pane in two parts: the goals done and not yet seen (a press opens a goal’s Result), and the goals ready for main, each ticked, with Release’s lich at the heading and one button, To main.

6 · Phone upright

Narrow · 390 × 844

The tabs are a row of heads with Stop all at the end; the Board’s columns are a head wide, their tasks tokens. The limits’ chips are in the Orkmaster’s … (press it to close or open the menu). The hand sits at the foot, clear of the home bar.

7 · Phone on its side

Low window · 844 × 390

The wide layout made compact: the tabs and the pane’s title on one line, smaller cards, the hand a fan with the deck at its left and the discard at its right. The hatched bands are the safe-area space at the sides.
