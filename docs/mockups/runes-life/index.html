Orkboard · a goal's rune from its summoning to main

1 · A stack: the goal on top, its flame below

Today a goal's rune stands three or four times on its stack: in the strip, on every edge of the fan and in the front card's header. The goal's name repeats too, under the strip, on the card the Orkmaster reads or routes. A card at work also moves twice: its stage row's dots run, and the head steps. Proposed: the strip holds the goal's rune and name, once. A card holds its task's name and no rune. An edge holds the stage's sign. While a task is at work, the goal's rune takes the place of the stage's sign, beside Doing · 3 m, and glows (from about 62 % to full and back, in steps, 2.4 s, no halo), and that replaces the running dots. A card that is the goal itself (the Orkmaster reading or routing it, no tasks yet) has no strip: its header is the goal's.

2 · Choosing the next goal's rune

On hover the + card shows the runes the addressed party does not use yet. The decorative runes give way, and the free elements' runes stand in the band, each in its colour, dim, one after another. The one the goal would be given stays at the top. When all five are in use, all five stand there. The choice itself is made in the field the card grows into, not on the leaning card: a 14 px rune on a card that turns under the pointer is too small a target, and too near the press that opens the field. The field has a row of the five runes at 28 px. The free ones are bright, the used ones faint but still possible, and the default is the one picked today. Arrows and Tab work, and doing nothing keeps today's rule.

3 · The rite: summoned at the +, sent through the ring

Pressing Send: the seal fills from the chosen rune, clockwise, in the goal's own colour, eight steps in 640 ms, and the + takes the colour for a moment. Then the card is drawn off the seal into its lane, and the colour leaves the seal. When the goal goes to main, the same rite runs in the portal's ring: the card goes in, and the ring's runes fill from its rune at the top, in its colour, and fade. It plays once each time, and never under reduced motion. It uses no gold: the goal's colour says this goal, as every card of it does.

This is a mockup and is not wired into the app. It is drawn in orkUI 0.20.0: the runes are the elements, and the page uses .ork-sigil, .ork-portal-ring and .ork-portal. The glow, the free runes in the band and the rite are this page's own, in orkUI's tokens. After the owner's yes they would go into orkUI first: an .ork-elem.is-glowing (a second thing that moves by itself, after .ork-busy), a seal that shows chosen runes, and the rite on the seal and on the ring.
