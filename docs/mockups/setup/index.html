2 · What the Orkmaster read

The Orkmaster reads the folder (names and counts only, spec 75 §1) and asks once whether it read it right. The signs it saw are the card's chips. It is something else opens the list of kinds in the card itself; an empty folder asks What is it for? in one line instead.

3 · The AI tools: the empty slot lifts the shelf

The Orkmaster asks who the orks run on, and the empty slot at the hand's right is lit. Pressing it lifts the shelf: every AI tool found on this computer, in the three groups the party's tool list already has. Use puts a tool in the hand, at the end of the row; the first one used is the main tool (framed). A tool is in the hand or on the shelf: there is no third state. The same shelf opens from the same slot every day after.

4 · A tool pressed: its own card

During the work, a press on a potion lifts that tool's card over the hand: how it is paid, whether it is the main tool, where it stands in the order the work moves on in, Check, and Put back. What used it (today's view) is one link on it. Taking a tool away takes effect between tasks, never inside one, and the card says what happens to a party set to it.

5 · The parties: the Orkmaster's large card on the table

The Orkmaster deals one large card onto the table: what the project is at its top (the list, read from the folder), then the stages, each party a row. A row is its leader and its team as heads, its size as three steps drawn in heads (Lean one, Standard four, Full the whole craft), its AI tool from the tools in the hand (the framed mark follows the main tool), and its price a goal. Pressing a head opens its seat (6). The card is the same later, from the empty chair and a party's Edit.

6 · A head pressed: its AI tool and its level

A press (hover only marks it) on any head of a row, the leader's too, opens its seat: its role, its AI tool (a list of the tools in the hand, the party's first), and its level as three heads, the same race in its three gears, each with its word and what a task costs at it. The leader's seat has no Take off. This is today's seat popover (seatpop.js), given the level.
