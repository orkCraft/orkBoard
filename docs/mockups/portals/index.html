Orkboard · the default tool and the portal to main

Decisions §177: the default tool’s potion stands in a frame that follows the open tab, and when it runs dry the work moves on to the next potion between tasks (the frame slides, the Orkmaster says it in one line); when every subscription is dry the Orkmaster asks, with a card, whether to go on paid; and a goal done is sent to main through a portal that opens above the discard (sealed, with its reason, while the gate holds the goal). Six cases, each at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 where it differs, with the potions drawn by orkUI’s own .ork-potion and --ork-mana (0.17.0).

1 · The default framed

Project’s tab · wide · 1280 × 800

Tech’s tab (Tech uses Codex) · wide, shorter · 1280 × 560

Phone · 390 × 844

Four tools (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor), each a potion under its own mark, in two rows of two (2 × 2) at the hand’s right edge. The default tool’s potion stands in a frame like a hotbar’s chosen slot: a 2 px line in the ink (orkUI’s --ork-ink, the corners cut by its notch), no colour, because a choice is not a signal. On the project’s tab the frame is on Claude Code, the machine’s main tool; on Tech’s tab (the second, shorter frame) it is on Codex, the tool Tech uses. The frame follows the open tab; the order left to right (then the next row) is the order work moves on in. The liquid is orkUI’s --ork-mana blue in all four, as every tool has room.

2 · Moving on

Wide · 1280 × 800

Phone · 390 × 844

Claude Code is down to its reserve (3%: one row of danger liquid, orkUI’s .is-danger; orkUI’s potion draws no separate reserve, so it is “just low”, with “at reserve” under it). The frame has slid to Codex: the frame it left is drawn dashed and faint on Claude Code’s potion, with one dashed, fainter frame half-way between, as a trail of the slide. The Orkmaster’s one line stands on the table, under the pane’s title, with its crowned head: the Orkmaster says what and who on the table, and the table is where a person looks first (a toast at the hand’s foot would sit on the potions it speaks about and be gone before it was read). It says “Claude Code is out until Thu 18:00: the work goes on with Codex.”; the line stays until the next thing happens, and moves only between tasks, never inside one.

3 · Every subscription dry

Wide · 1280 × 800

Phone · 390 × 844

All four subscription potions are empty (0%, in danger; each tool’s reset is in its words on hover, and the soonest, Claude Code’s Thu 18:00, is the one the card says). The frame stays on Claude Code: nothing has moved, as no tool has room. The tool paid by its API is not a potion, since it has no limit to pour: it is its coloured mark with orkUI’s coins icon in a dashed box where the potion stands, the word “API” under it and “≈$0.40” a task under that; it joins the row at the end while the question is open. The Orkmaster’s card is lifted as a card (the v3 lifted card: its frame, its name, the window with the coins sign and the crowned head, the party and the price as a gem, the body between the window and the foot, the foot turned 1.5°) from the hand’s first stack, over the lanes; Go on, paid is orkUI’s action button, Wait for Thu 18:00 the quiet one.

4 · The portal

Wide · 1280 × 800

The portal alone, at its size · A in the play green (the main version, as in the frame) · B in ink only, no colour, to compare

The person has pressed (or begun to drag) Lobby in the discard, which is ready for main: the portal has opened in the right column above the discard. It is round and magical, not a door. It is drawn on a grid of 5 px cells, only from orkUI’s tokens: a ring of pixel stones (--ork-ink and --ork-edge, with gaps in --ork-frame) round a dark inner lip, and inside it a swirl of three arms turning in to a dark eye, with a few pixel sparks just outside the ring. The swirl’s light is --ork-action, orkUI’s green: the portal is where an action is played, so the green is the signal “you can play it here”, and it is the only colour on the portal. The swirl turns by itself, slowly: a stepped quarter turn every 1.5 s, six seconds a turn, the swirl alone (the ring and the sparks stand still) so the pixels stay crisp; none under reduced motion. Which is which: A (left, and the one in the frame) has the swirl in the play green; B (right) is the same portal in ink only, no colour, if colour should be kept for states. While a card is held over it, as here, the swirl is brighter (--ork-action-hi) and the ring a step wider: it takes the card. Its label “Main” is the pixel face, the one place the pixel face is used here besides the buttons. Over it the line says before it acts what goes: “Into main: Lobby, and Reconnect it stands on”. The Lobby card is shown mid-drag, turned 6° (orkUI’s lean) with a hard pixel shadow, between the pile (its top card is now Menu) and the portal, with a dashed ink path and an arrow. At its left stands the first-time hint “Drag a goal done into the portal to send it to main”; the portal shimmers once when it opens (a stepped flash over the swirl, none under reduced motion).

5 · The portal sealed

Wide · 1280 × 800

Phone · 390 × 844

The gate holds Lobby: Tech’s check is red on dev (the card “Check the seat” on the table is red too). The portal is still round, but stilled and grey: the swirl stands (no turning, no green) in --ork-frame and --ork-ink-faint, the ring is dim (--ork-edge and --ork-frame) and the sparks are gone. A pixel seal lies across it, two bars crossed in an X over a small knot (orkUI has no seal sign, so it is drawn in its tokens: --ork-ink-faint bars with a dark edge), its label dimmed. Its reason stands under it in the danger colour: “Sealed: Tech’s check failed on dev”. The goal stays in the pile, taken (orkUI’s .ork-picked brackets), because pressing it only explained why.

6 · The portal on a phone

Phone · 390 × 844

On the phone the discard lies at the lanes’ end; the person has tapped Lobby (taken, in orkUI’s brackets) and the portal has opened above it in the same column: the same round portal, with its swirl turning in the play green, drawn in 4 px cells so it is 104 px wide. The line “Into main: Lobby, and Reconnect it stands on” is over it, and under it the text “Tap the portal to send it to main” with an arrow up to it. There is no drag on a phone: a tap on the portal sends the goal.
