1 · At rest: nothing is ready for main

The window at 1280 × 800, on the project's tab, with four parties. Release and Live are not tabs. They stand at the table's end, on the road after the work, read from the bottom up: the discard (done in dev), Release (the lich and its skeletons, who take a goal to main), the portal (main), Live (the imp and its goblins, who run what people use). Each station is a row of small heads with its label. Press one to open that party's view over the pane, as the discard opens. At rest the stations are still, the ring sleeps, and the discard is its place. The tabs carry each party's sword and shield: the marks of the tools its Does and its Checks run on, in ink, the open tab's in colour. One rune per stack, and the rune glows at the stage of a task at work.

Tabs: Game design does and checks on Claude Code; Tech does on Claude Code and checks on Codex; Design is Lean, so its shield is faint with no mark (the doer runs its own tests); Marketing does on Codex, with a dot because one of its roles runs on another tool. Lanes: two runes glow, one in Tech (the timer being reset) and one in Design (the bar's colours being checked).

2 · A goal ready, Release at work, an alert from Live

A goal is ready for main, so the ring is open, and the goal chosen in the discard's view puts its rune (lava) at the ring's top. The lich steps, as a tab's head does while its party works. Live has an alert, the most urgent thing on the table, because it is what people use. The pointer is on Claude's potion: the tabs that drink from it are marked (the Does of Game design and Tech, and the Checks of Game design).

3 · A new goal on Tech's tab

On Tech's tab, Tech's sword and shield are in colour. The + card was hovered (the free runes, void and lava, stood in its band), then pressed: the field holds the row of runes. Void is chosen; meadow and ice are Tech's goals' runes, faint but possible. On Send the seal fills from void in its colour, and the card is drawn into Tech's lane (the rite: runes-life, 3).

4 · The same at 1024 × 768

Three lanes fit under the tabs. The road keeps its four places, but the window is 32 px shorter: the label Main gives way (the ring and its line say it), and the discard's foot tucks further under the hand, which already stands over the pile. Below about 740 px tall the stations would have to move beside the ring; that is a limit to note in the spec. The tabs' second line still fits (the tab is 52 px tall).

5 · The lighter way, for comparison

Release and Live stay tabs, but sit at the tabs' right end, over the table's end, with a gap before them. The tabs and their signals stay as they are, but the lanes gain nothing and the road from the discard to Live is only hinted at. Shown for the choice; I would take 1.

This is a mockup and is not wired into the app. It is drawn in orkUI 0.20.0 (the runes are the elements; .ork-sigil, .ork-portal-ring, .ork-portal, .ork-potion, the heads and the tools' marks in colour and in ink). The rite and the choice of a rune are in docs/mockups/runes-life/. Open it over http, not file://.
