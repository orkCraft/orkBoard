1 · A party's lens, today and proposed

The owner: the leaders are your fellow players at the table (decisions §199.2). So the open tab is the player you are looking through: its head large (48 × 44, the race sheet's @2x, §161), its alerts and notes beside its head instead of a strip over the map, and the quick question a bubble from its head instead of a line always open. The party's header loses Leads (the tab is the leader), and becomes one row: Does with orkUI's sword, Checks with its shield, each seat's role under its head, the leader's aspects at the row's right. The tab grows in height only: a tab is its column's head on the Board (§143), so its width never changes.

Who decides sits at the table; who does is the leader's squad. The leaders sit on chairs at the table's far edge (the tabs, over the table's rim), the other leaders at 36 × 33, the open one leaning in at 48 × 44. The squad stays at 24 × 22 (the smallest size a head is drawn crisp at with its tier's gear readable, §161; 12 × 11 would break §148.7's 16 px), and is made lighter instead: the AI tool's mark once by the sword and once by the shield (a head carries its own only when its seat runs on another tool), the role in the muted caption. A member at work is its small head on the card it works on (Backend on index.ts). + Party is the empty chair.

2 · The table: the leaders on their chairs, the empty chair

Through the Orkmaster's tab the Board is the table itself: each leader's lane is the table in front of its chair, the cards it plays lying before it, and a member at work is a small head on the card it works on, as a worker placed on a board. The Orkmaster's chair is like the others; its crown says who leads the table. The empty chair is New party: the whole chair, faint, nobody on it; hovered or focused it is pulled out a little; pressed, the one place of the parties opens as + Party opens it today (§151.5). It stands only while a craft is not at the table: a full table has no empty chair, and nothing has to say no more. It takes a narrower column and no lane. Release and Live keep their places in the portal's corners (§188): they come to the table only when there is something to do. The word stays New party: seat is the squad's word (a member's place), so the chair is a picture, never a new word.

Dark edges (the owner: the back behind the chairs black, and the space behind the hand too): the room is dark and the table between lit, a step lighter, with its rim on both sides. The table is what is shared (the Board, the leaders' cards, the discard, the portal); the dark edges are the players' places: the leaders across it on their chairs, the person on the near side with the hand. The + card does not lose its sense: it is a card of the person's hand, and sent it leaves the dark for the lit table, face down in the Orkmaster's lane (spec 120 §6.2): played, seen. Nothing glows: the lamp is only a step of grey. The dark is this page's token until orkUI draws a step under its canvas (never #000).

3 · The bubble: the one way a leader speaks

Pressed, the leader's head opens a bubble under it with the field focused (Ask the Architect…); Enter sends, Esc or a press away closes it, the text kept. The same bubble holds the answer (the question muted over it, the sources, Make it a goal, ×), the evening line once a day (spec 121 E), and the leader's side in a hearing (spec 120 §6.7): whatever a leader says comes out of its head, in one shape. Its foot names the leader's seat (Claude Code · Elder): pressed, the seat's popover as today, since the head itself now asks. Only a leader and the Orkmaster speak: a member never (§110.8). On the phone there is no quick question (§181.8).

4 · The faces: a state, never an idle motion

A leader's face says what happened to its party, and then stands still: §149.1's no idle motion holds (a tool looked at all day never sways), so nothing blinks or breathes on a timer. A face changes only when its party's state does, and a change made while the page was hidden is simply there on coming back (§89.0). The face is an accent: the alert sign and the words stay (a 24 px face is not a sign). Only the leaders and the Orkmaster have faces; a member keeps one. Under reduced motion the faces still change (a swap is not a motion), the head's one beat does not play. Sketched here on the heads' own 12 × 11 grid, a few cells each; orkUI draws them properly, then they are copied.

5 · The narrow width (390 × 760)

The tabs are a row of heads (as today); the open one is a step larger (36 × 33) with its ! and dot beside it. The header is the same one row, wrapping: the sword's seats, the shield's, the aspects under them. No chairs at this width (a 52 px tab has no room for one): the empty chair is a small sign before Stop, New party its label. The bubble spans the width under the heads. Above the map: measured on the right.

6 · Rules kept, and what this changes

  • The frame stays (§104.0, §145): no new pane, column, drawer or dialog; the bubble is the head's popover, as a seat's is (§110.7).
  • A tab's width never changes (§143): the open leader grows the tab row's height by about 16 px, not its column.
  • No count (§67, §145.3): the strip's 1 alert, 2 notes goes; the head shows the alert sign and the note's dot, a press steps through, the numbers live only in the label (The Architect: 1 alert, 2 notes).
  • A member never speaks (§110.8): no bubble, no face but its one, its questions its leader's.
  • No idle motion (§149.1): faces are states; the head's beat plays once, when it hands a card (spec 120 §6.9's give) or answers; nothing under reduced motion.
  • One look (orkUI's office): the bubble on the raised panel with the edge, the faces drawn in orkUI first, nothing of the page's own colour.
  • Dark edges: a step under orkUI's canvas for the leaders' side and the hand, the table lifted a step between, its rim on both sides; no glow, no gradient, never #000; the faint ink holds AA on the dark. Made in orkUI first (a token), then copied.
  • The chair is a thing drawn at a sign's weight, as orkUI's banner, boards, die and potions are: the frame's greys, no colour, no legs, never a picture of furniture; still at rest. orkUI draws it (the occupied back and the empty chair), then it is copied.
  • Who decides at the table, who does in the squad (§104.0): leaders 36–48 px with faces and a voice; members 24 px, no face, no voice, their place the leader's lens and the cards they work on; never a tab of their own (§151.4).
  • Changes: §110.6 and spec 95 §2.5 (the header's Leads goes: the tab is the leader; Does and Checks wear the sword and the shield of the tabs and the party's editor, §151.5; each seat's role under its head); spec 106 §3 (the line Ask the … becomes the leader's bubble); spec 108 §3 (the strip over the map: its counts to the head, Ask again to the head's menu); spec 109 §4 (the aspects' row joins the header's row); §149.4 (the leaders' and the Orkmaster's heads gain five faces each, in orkUI); the quick question's answer, the evening line and a hearing's side share one bubble; spec 95 §2.2–§2.3 (the tabs: a leader's head at 36, the open one at 48, on a chair; + New party becomes the empty chair, before the limits and Stop all); §192.4 and tabmarks.js (the tool's mark once a side in the header, on a head only where it differs).
  • Not here: a member's task under its head (the header shows who works, never their tasks, §110.8); a leader's Now line (gone, spec 95 §10); voices (spec 122).
