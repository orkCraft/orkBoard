1 · The window, played

The Orkmaster's column stays on every tab: the goals, top to bottom by time (the one whose spec it writes, those at work, Next, Tonight). The tabs are whose eyes: the Board shows each leader's tasks, and Tech's and Design's tabs show their maps. Press a goal in the column: every tab looks at it. The Board lights its tasks, and a leader's map zooms to the parts it touches, its spec at that leader's level beside it. Esc or ✕ looks at the whole project again. The + card at the hand's left still turns over where it lies.

The column is the first lane, pinned. It holds the goals, one card each, and it does not change when the tab does. A goal lies only here; its tasks lie only in its leaders' lanes. Nothing is drawn twice: here the goal, there its tasks, joined by the rune and by the pick.

At work: the goal whose spec the Orkmaster writes is a whole card, its ring running. The goals at work at the leaders are strips with the heads of who works on them. When the spec is written, its card becomes a strip and its tasks are dealt to the leaders' lanes.

Next and Tonight are strips, by priority. The first of Next has its wait as a thin bar in eighths, and starts by itself when the Orkmaster is free. A press reads it, with Start now and Tonight.

A goal's state shows in the column whatever the tab: a yellow ? when it waits for you, a faint ? when it asked another leader, red when a task failed. A question flies from the task's card on the Board, or from the goal's strip on another tab.

The zoom: a leader's tab looks at the whole project, or, with a goal picked, at that goal at its own level. Tech shows the parts of the system it touches, its contract and its Done when; Design its screens and their states. It is not a separate lens: the pick decides what every tab shows.

The hand gets only what needs you: a choice only a person makes, a question no leader could answer, a failure. A question another leader can answer goes through Passing on in the column, where you can take it.

2 · The seal: a whole ring

The ring has eight places. Today, while the runes are offered, the decorations give way and only five places are lit, so the ring has gaps. Proposed: the five runes take five places, and orkUI's own three runes keep the other three. The ring is whole, and its eight places are also the wait's eight steps.

3 · Corners that grow with the card

Today every card has the same 2 px corner, so a large card reads as a plain rectangle. Proposed: a quarter circle in 2 px cells, about one cell for every 40 px of width. A small card hardly changes, and a large one is plainly a card. The line follows the steps.

4 · At work, asks, failed

Today the glow goes from 62 % to full, on a 14 px rune, and it stops under reduced motion. That is easy to miss. Proposed: from 30 % to full, the ring running round on a back at work, and the state said in words under the card, so it is there without motion too. Moving means at work, yellow and still means it waits for you, red means it failed and is in your hand.

This is a mockup and is not wired into the app. It is drawn with orkUI's tokens and pieces: .ork-card, .ork-plus, .ork-sigil (with .is-waiting), .ork-elem, .ork-rune, .ork-sign, .ork-potion, .ork-btn, .ork-field.
