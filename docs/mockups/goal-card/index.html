1 · The window, played

Press the + card at the hand's left end. It turns over where it lies and grows up from there, its foot under the window's edge. Esc or a press away turns it back, keeping its words. The squares under its top open their choices in a row, as the rune does. Send turns it to its back, and it flies to the Orkmaster's Next pile. Hover a pile to fan it and read each card's name. Press the top of Next to read its spec.

At work is the goal whose spec the Orkmaster writes now: face down, its ring runs round and its rune glows. Under it, in words: Writing the spec. Under reduced motion the ring stands lit at one place, and the words still say it.

Next is the queue, by priority, the first on top. Its ring is the wait: it fills in eighths, and when it is whole the goal goes to work by itself as soon as the Orkmaster is free. A press on it reads the spec, with Start now and Tonight.

Tonight waits for the night's window, with no ring. A goal moves there from Next with one press.

A question leaves the goal: the card stays at its leader, yellow and still, and the question flies out of it into the hand. Answered, it flies back into the goal. When none is left, the yellow goes.

A question another leader can answer does not come to you. It flies from its goal to the Orkmaster's Passing on, stays there a few seconds where you can see it (a press: Answer it myself or Pass it now), then goes to that leader, and the answer flies back into the goal. The goal never moves: it stays its leader's, and says Asked Tech. It is not yellow, because yellow means only one thing: you are needed.

The hand gets only what needs you: a choice only a person makes (the look, money, the direction, anything that cannot be undone), a question no leader could answer, and a failure.

A failure cannot go on at its leader: the card turns red and is thrown into the hand from where it lay. A dashed place stays in the lane, so Try again has somewhere to go back to.

The name on a back is a plate at its foot: the rune and the short name, two lines. In a pile only the top's plate shows. Hovered, the pile fans and each card under shows its plate. The whole title is in the card's words for a screen reader and in its tooltip.

2 · The seal: a whole ring

The ring has eight places. Today, while the runes are offered, the decorations give way and only five places are lit, so the ring has gaps. Proposed: the five runes take five places, and orkUI's own three runes keep the other three. The ring is whole, and its eight places are also the wait's eight steps.

3 · Corners that grow with the card

Today every card has the same 2 px corner, so a large card reads as a plain rectangle. Proposed: a quarter circle in 2 px cells, about one cell for every 40 px of width. A small card hardly changes, and a large one is plainly a card. The line follows the steps.

4 · At work, asks, failed

Today the glow goes from 62 % to full, on a 14 px rune, and it stops under reduced motion. That is easy to miss. Proposed: from 30 % to full, the ring running round on a back at work, and the state said in words under the card, so it is there without motion too. Moving means at work, yellow and still means it waits for you, red means it failed and is in your hand.
