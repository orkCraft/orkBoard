1 · The window, played

The Orkmaster's column stays on every tab, off the table on the canvas's black, as the hand is. It holds two piles of whole cards: At work (the goal whose spec it writes in front, its ring running, and under it the goals at the leaders) and Next, one pile in line where each card says when it starts (in seconds for the first, a time for the rest, the moon and 01:00 for the night's). Hover a card of a pile: it comes out whole. The tabs are whose eyes: the Board shows each leader's tasks; Tech's and Design's tabs show their maps of the whole project. Press a goal: a leader's tab turns to another view, what that goal changes at its level (its parts, new, changed or read only; the contract's lines; the screens now and as they will be), with where it lies on the whole map beside it. Esc or ✕ looks at the whole project again.

Whole cards, no strips. A pile shows its front card whole and the foot of each card under it: the card's own frame, its rune, its short name and what it waits for. More than four: thin edges, no number. A card hovered or focused slides out of its pile whole, with its whole name.

One pile in line. Tonight was a start time, not a place: the night's goals lie at the pile's end under the moon. A press reads a card: Start now, Tonight, or for a night's card Today, in turn. The first of the day's starts by itself when its ring is whole and the Orkmaster is free.

A goal lies only in the column, its tasks only in its leaders' lanes, joined by the rune and the pick. When its spec is written its card goes under the front of At work and its tasks are dealt to the lanes. A goal's state shows on its card in the column whatever the tab: yellow when it waits for you, a faint ? when it asked another leader, red when a task failed.

Two views at a leader's tab. The whole project is the leader's map: what is. A goal picked is what it will change: Tech's parts of the system each marked new, changed or read only, with its lines (+ added, ~ changed) and the contract's diff; Design's screens now and as they will be, with their states. Beside it, a small whole map with its parts lit, its Done when, its tasks there.

Off the table. The canvas and the table are near in colour; what makes the table read is its rim (orkUI's .ork-table). The column and the hand lie off it, on the canvas: the Orkmaster's and yours, around the leaders' play. Column: off the table above switches it, to compare.

The hand gets only what needs you: a choice only a person makes, a question no leader could answer, a failure. A question another leader can answer passes over the column (passing on), where you can take it.

2 · The seal: a whole ring

The ring has eight places. Today, while the runes are offered, the decorations give way and only five places are lit, so the ring has gaps. Proposed: the five runes take five places, and orkUI's own three runes keep the other three. The ring is whole, and its eight places are also the wait's eight steps.

3 · Corners that grow with the card

Today every card has the same 2 px corner, so a large card reads as a plain rectangle. Proposed: a quarter circle in 2 px cells, about one cell for every 40 px of width. A small card hardly changes, and a large one is plainly a card. The line follows the steps.

4 · At work, asks, failed

Today the glow goes from 62 % to full, on a 14 px rune, and it stops under reduced motion. That is easy to miss. Proposed: from 30 % to full, the ring running round on a back at work, and the state said in words under the card, so it is there without motion too. Moving means at work, yellow and still means it waits for you, red means it failed and is in your hand.

This is a mockup and is not wired into the app. It is drawn with orkUI's tokens and pieces: .ork-card, .ork-plus, .ork-sigil (with .is-waiting), .ork-elem, .ork-rune, .ork-sign, .ork-potion, .ork-btn, .ork-field.
