Orkboard · a goal’s way, and the one place of the parties · mockup s2b

Drawn on S1’s frame, with S1’s hand: three places (the deck with the + card drawn off it, the stacks, the discard), one size of card, answers only on a lifted card.

1 · The route card

Wide · 1280 × 800

The goal Results screen with the round’s best answer has just been sent from the blank card. The Orkmaster routed it by code; its route card is lifted from the hand over the pane’s foot: the leaders’ heads in order, each with a small ×, a + for any party but Release, the cost, its place in line, and one button, Talk. Nothing has been called yet. The three parties on the route carry a dot on their tabs.

2 · The route card, limits running low

Wide · 1280 × 800

The same card when the week’s limits run low: Later, by itself stands beside Talk. Pressed, it puts the goal in the deck, waiting for the reset.

3 · A bug’s route card

Wide · 1280 × 800

A bug goes to Tech alone, and a goal of one party opens on that party’s tab. Left: the short way when there is no failing test yet, whose Talk is the diagnosis. Right: the variant when the report already holds a failing test, with Start and The long way as a quiet link. Both are shown in one frame; in the window only one of them is ever lifted.

4 · Talk: a goal’s stack of questions

Wide · 1280 × 800

The pane is the goal’s page at Talk, on the Orkmaster’s tab: the three leaders’ stances in short, each with a for and an against. In the hand, the goal’s stack: its top card, the first question, is lifted with its leader’s head, the choices with the pick and its why, Other…, and Let Game design decide at the stack’s foot. The edges under the stack hint at the questions left.

5 · The decision’s card

Wide · 1280 × 800

When Talk closes, the Orkmaster words the decision: its number, its title, Why, three numbered points and one button, Confirm and start. No cost gem: it spends nothing new.

6 · A fork

Wide · 1280 × 800

At Spec every leader’s section is on the pane. Where two disagree the Orkmaster does not choose: a fork is lifted, both sides with their leaders’ heads and their whys, a choice each, and Other….

7 · A bug’s diagnosis

Wide · 1280 × 800

The bug’s Talk is Tech’s guess of the cause: its card is lifted with Yes, No, it’s elsewhere and Other…. The pane is Tech’s System with server/lobby.ts lit. No decision is written for a bug.

8 · The project’s questions

Wide · 1280 × 800

A stack of no goal’s, dealt once at the first start, with Game design’s leader’s head on it (in a game Game design asks). Its picks are read from the project’s README. It holds nothing back: the Board and every goal go on without it.

9 · The one place of the parties

Wide · 1280 × 800

Over the pane, from the first start, the tabs’ + or a party’s …. At the left: the project’s kind read from the project, and the parties under the stages’ headings; Release is ticked and locked. Tech pressed opens at the right: its size, its leader’s row, Does and Checks as role cards, and at the foot the cost together and Save. orkUI’s copy has no sword or shield sign yet, so Does and Checks are plain labels here.

10 · Phone upright: Talk

Narrow · 390 × 844

State 4 on the phone: the stances stacked on the pane, and the question lifted as a sheet from the foot of the table, ending above the hand.
