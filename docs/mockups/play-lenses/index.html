1 · Play under Design's tab: the Product designer's lens

The made-up project is Sproutly, a phone-first web app that reminds you to water your plants. The die is playing; Play lies over the pane. The open tab is Design, so the strip under Play's bar is the Product designer's: where this page is on its map (Today · Step 1), its rubric and its aspects for this screen only. On the frame, a concern of Accessibility (the Watered buttons are 20 px tall) and a hint the probe saw (A press did nothing on Snooze). The concern is pressed: its back is lifted beside the frame, with Make it a goal and Not a problem. The marks let the product's presses through.

Strip: the aspects' states are the rubric's (spec 109), counted only on this screen's parts; a press on one steps through its marks here. Hint vs concern: a hint is what code saw (a dotted outline, a hollow square), a concern what the leader said (a solid outline, its head).

3 · Under Product's tab: the Product manager's lens

4 · Mark: a bug, under Tech's tab

Mark (or M) lays a still layer on the frame: the product takes no press, the bar says Draw a box or press a part · Cancel. A box is drawn over the second plant's Watered. The field opens beside it, inside the pane: the line, the kind (Bug), → Tech (the open tab's; @ changes it), and With: what goes with it, each with its × (here the server's last lines are taken off). With an error and a failed request kept, the bug reproduces: Tech alone, the short way, no diagnosis call.
