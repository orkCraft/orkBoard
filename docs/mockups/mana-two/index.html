Orkboard · the mana in two windows: the liquid is now, the bottle is the week

1 · Four bottles for the week

A subscriber asks two things: can I work now? (the 5-hour window) and will I last until the reset? (the week). One potion answers both: the liquid is the shorter window, how much is at hand now, and the bottle is the longer one, how much can still be carried this week. The bottle shrinks in four drawn sizes, a game's potions (the large is orkUI's potion today, 14 × 18; then 12 × 14, 10 × 10 and a vial 8 × 9), never scaled, so every one stays on the 3 px grid: above 60 % of the week left the large, 30 to 60 % the medium, 10 to 30 % the small, under 10 % the vial. The week drains slowly, so four steps are enough.

2 · The potions in the hand

Each tool keeps the largest bottle's place, the bottle standing on its floor, so the row never moves and the room left above a small bottle says the week has thinned. Two shares under it: the now share in bold (in the warning's or the danger's colour at their levels), the week's under it, muted. The colour follows whichever runs out first: a vial brim-full is still red, because the week is what will stop it. A tool with one window keeps the large bottle and its level; a tool paid by its API keeps its coin.

Claude Code, the default: 30% of the 5 hours left (until 18:40), 82% of the week (until Thu 09:00). Game design and Tech do on it.

3 · A week, on one tool

Monday to Thursday morning, one potion: the liquid empties and fills again every five hours; the bottle steps down through the week, and at the weekly reset it is the large one again, full.
