1 · The window: the table, the hand and the table's edge

What a tab shows is its table (here Tech's table; never map, lens or view). Only the table scrolls, as one, its lanes together; the tabs and the hand stay. The portal and the discard leave the table: at its right, one strip of objects, top to bottom, the portal's ring with the imp at twelve (Live) and the lich at six (Release), the discard, the die, the potions at the hand's level. No list, no count. The table gains the width they took.

① One road. Done goals lie in the discard; To main flies them up into the ring, where they go in at the lich. What comes out at twelve is live, the imp's.

② The ring is always drawn from Start, faint and still while nothing is ready for main, its heads asleep (§247.1 changed: the width it saved is no longer the table's).

③ The die under the discard, over the potions. Play on its up face, Stop all in front, Resume already waiting on its right face (2).

④ The scrollbar is the table's alone. The column never scrolls (§251.4); the hand is cut under its cards' titles.

2 · The die turns a quarter

The face seen is what a press does. Resume lies on the right face from the start, muted, seen at the side, so the turn shows where it comes from. Stop all pressed, the cube turns a quarter about its up axis and Resume is the front; Resume pressed, it turns back. The words under it say the state. Play's mark is drawn upright after the turn. The Board turns it, never the person.

4 · A conflict is a card the Orkmaster throws

The decision's call sees that the new goal goes against a standing decision. Before Confirm and start, so nothing of the spec is paid, the Orkmaster throws a card of the kind conflict into the hand under the first lane. Lifted, the earlier decision comes out of the portal, from the lich, and lies beside it, both with their why.

5 · The past through whose eyes: the portal holds it, the open tab says whose

The chronicle is the lich's table, read on the frame's two axes (what is looked at · through whom): the lich pressed opens the past through the open tab. On Design's tab it deals Design's past decisions and goals; on the Orkmaster's, the whole project's. The line under the lich says whose past it holds (Design's past), so the portal is found by its place. On the lich's own table the only choice is a deck a version, Unreleased on top, and a search; no heads to pick, the tab already picked one. The ring itself stays the way into main (pressed only while something is ready); the imp is Live.

At the portal: the lich's fan, the open tab's last decisions

The owner's way in, before any press: at the lich, up to three small cards come out of the portal, the last decisions of the open tab's craft (on the Orkmaster's tab, the project's). At 56 × 78 a card says its rune, its kind and its number; hovered, it rises whole with its title. It moves only for what is new since that craft's past was last looked at, else it lies still (one place moves at a time, §247.8). Pressed (the fan or the lich), the past opens over the pane, laid by version, as below. Main comes first: while something is ready for main the ring is the gate's, and the fan tucks in behind the lich.

Or out of the portal itself: a stack, or a pyramid going into it

The owner's two other ways: the past comes out of the ring itself, not beside the lich. The stack: a squared pile in the portal's eye, the newest face up on top, the edges for the rest (five at most, §67). The pyramid: the cards step back into the swirl, each older one smaller and deeper, so the depth is the age. In both, while something is ready for main the cards sink into the portal and the ring is the gate's; the lich at six opens the past.

7 · The first open: four cards thrown

The room dark, the Orkmaster in its middle holding four backs. Each card flies to its place, and the place appears as it lands: the potions, the portal, the table, the hand. A card whose thing is already set is not thrown. No tour: the fourth card is the person's first real goal, its corners lit one at a time as they fill.

8 · Words written to fit

Every call that words a card is given its budget: a title up to seven words, a window's words up to three lines, a lifted card one sentence. The page clamps as a net only; the whole words are on the lifted card.
