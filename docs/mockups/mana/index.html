Orkboard · the limits as the hand’s mana

The AI tools’ limits lie in the hand as mana, each tool a potion flush against the window’s right edge. Three changes since the last version: the liquid is a dim mana blue while a tool has room (a stand-in for a proposed orkUI token, --ork-mana; alert at 20% or less, danger under 10%); the discard has left the hand and lies on the table, at the foot of its last column; and a lifted card is a card, twice a hand card, held up where its stack lay. Two cases, each at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844, and the potion.

1 · Two tools

Wide · 1280 × 800

Phone · 390 × 844

Two tools in one row at the hand’s right edge, alone there: the hand holds the + card and its stacks, which now have the discard’s width. Claude Code (62%) is mana blue, with its top hatched for the lifted goal that spends 4% and “−4%” said under it; Codex (18%) is alert. The discard lies on the table, at the foot of the last column right of the lanes: its top card a goal done (its stage sign is Done), two backs fanned under it, “Discard” over it. The lifted goal is a card, twice a hand card (288 px wide, 5 : 7), turned 1.5°, its foot on the hand’s line where its stack lay, over the lanes above; what the panel held is its body between the window and the foot. On the phone the discard lies at the lanes’ end, at the top of the free column, where it reads best and the lifted card (the screen’s width less 16 px, rising from the hand) never covers it.

2 · Six tools

Wide · 1280 × 800

Phone · 390 × 844

Six tools (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor, Hermes, Pi) in two rows of three, each potion a little smaller (2 px a cell, the hand a little taller). Hermes at 7% runs danger, Codex at 18% alert, the other four are mana blue. The wide case is Tech’s tab: the party’s map fills the table’s last column and a note-sized strip of it is drawn above the discard, so the map is seen to end above it. The lifted card here is a decision with a long body (a Why and four numbered points): the body scrolls inside the card, its buttons stay at its foot, and the card never grows wider. On the phone the same card is the screen’s width less 16 px, the discard at the lanes’ end, the same 3 × 2 potions at the hand’s end.

3 · The potion

The potion on its 14 × 18 grid at its levels, drawn at twice the size: full, with 4% being spent (the top of the liquid hatched), half, low (alert) and under 10% (danger). The liquid is the mana blue (the stand-in --mana, a proposed orkUI --ork-mana) while the tool has room, above 20%; the shine on its left is the same blue lightened a little toward the ink. Hover says “Claude Code: 62 % of the week left”; a press would open what used it over the pane.
