1 · One card, 5 : 7, cut by its place

Before 4 November every card was 5 : 7: the column's 112 × 157, the hand's 144 × 202, the lifted 404 × 566, and the column's back the same card on its side. §249 cut them to their words. Proposed: the shape comes back and stays, and a place cuts the card instead of changing it: the column's window edge cuts a face-down card's left, the hand's window edge cuts every card's foot. A face-up card always stands. A card lies on its side only face down, in analysis, not yet agreed (§206.3, §206.11): lying keeps that meaning. The crop lines are part of the card's template: what lies past one is the card's secondary content, never an empty half, never a line cut in two.

Today a face-up card is 112 wide and as tall as its words (§249.1). 2.3 · B: a goal comes to your hand

In B the column's card and the hand's are one card at one size: it slides out of its pile and down into the hand, cut at 128 like the others, and its face changes on the way from the column's (rune, name, who has it) to the hand's (the kind, who asks, the title). In A it grows from 112 to 144 on the way. Under reduced motion it is in the hand at once.

2.4 · The opened card: whole, and it never grows

The lifted card is 404 × 566 today already. One rule is added: the card keeps its shape whatever it holds. A long question scrolls inside its body (the fade at its foot says there is more); the answer, the field and the buttons stay at its foot, always in reach. Same corner and title as the hand's card, so the card that rose is the card that opened.

3 · Phone

The phone's hand card is 80 × 112 already, 5 : 7, cut at 82 px. What changes there is what lies under the cut (today nothing), and that the crown's piles and the opened card take the same shapes as on the desktop (the piles drawn in B). A touch has no hover: a card pressed rises and shows its lower part, a card tapped opens. The one place that leaves 5 : 7 is the opened card while the keyboard is up: 5 : 7 at a phone's width is 316 × 442, and a keyboard takes 250 px of a 700 px screen.
