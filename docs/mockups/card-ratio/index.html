1 · One card, 5 : 7 where it is held or opened

Before 4 November every card was 5 : 7; §249 cut them to their words. Proposed: a card is 5 : 7 wherever it is held or opened (the hand, the discard, the lanes, the opened card), and a place cuts it only where something covers it: the window's bottom edge cuts the hand's cards, the window's left edge a face-down card, the card in front the cards behind it. A face-up card always stands; a card lies on its side only face down, not yet agreed (§206.3, §206.11). The Orkmaster's column says where a goal is and must never scroll, so its face-up card stays as tall as its words, now the hand's width: when the goal comes to you, it opens to the hand's 5 : 7 on the way.

2 · Desktop

2.1 · The Orkmaster's column: the hand's width, as tall as its words

Today a face-up card is 112 wide and a face-down card 157 × 112 with 45 px under the window's edge; the column is 128 px. Proposed: the face-up card is 144 wide, the hand's, so the name takes three lines of about 18 characters (about 55 in all, today about 40); it stays as tall as its words (about 86 px). The back keeps its 5 : 7 on its side and lies 13 px under the edge, so both piles keep one right edge (§236). The column is 160 px; the table 32 px narrower. Hover a card to slide it out.

2.2 · The column never scrolls: its piles give way

A pile shows its front card whole. Each card behind it shows its foot (20 px: its wait or its heads) while the window has room; when it has not, the cards behind give way to a thin edge (3 px), the furthest first, and never a scrollbar: the column is a glance, and what lies under a scroll is not seen. Nothing counts what waits (the edges say more lie here). Drag the window's height: 650 is a 1366 × 768 laptop's browser, 790 a 1440 × 900.

2.3 · The hand: 5 : 7, cut at its foot

Today the card is what its anatomy needs, 144 × 128, whole (§249.2). Proposed: the card is 144 × 202 and the window's edge cuts it at the same 128, so the hand is exactly as tall as today and the table loses nothing. Hovered, the card rises by 74 px and shows its lower part: why it is asked (two lines, the leader's own reason), how long it waits and what it holds (tasks stopped behind it, or what it costs). Hover a card in each row.

2.4 · A goal comes to your hand: the card opens to 5 : 7

The column's card and the hand's are one width, 144. When a goal comes to you its card slides out of its pile, goes down into the hand and opens to the hand's 5 : 7 on the way, cut at 128 like the others; its face changes from the column's (rune, name, wait) to the hand's (the kind, who asks, the title). Under reduced motion it is in the hand at once.

2.5 · The opened card: whole, and it never grows

The lifted card is 404 × 566 today already. One rule is added: the card keeps its shape whatever it holds. A long question scrolls inside its body (the fade at its foot says there is more); the answer, the field and the buttons stay at its foot, always in reach. Same corner and title as the hand's card, so the card that rose is the card that opened.

3 · Phone

The phone's hand card is 80 × 112 already, 5 : 7, cut at 82 px. What changes there is what lies under the cut (today nothing), and that the crown's piles take the column's new cards. The crown's piles are a screen of their own on the phone, a list, and that list may scroll. A touch has no hover: a card pressed rises and shows its lower part, a card tapped opens. The one place that leaves 5 : 7 is the opened card while the keyboard is up: 5 : 7 at a phone's width is 316 × 442, and a keyboard takes 250 px of a 700 px screen.

Scroll on the desktop only on the table and inside an opened card. The phone's crown's piles are a list of their own and may scroll.

New in the card's template: the hand card's lower part (why, waits, holds), read from what the Board has: the question's own words, its time asked, the tasks whose step waits on it, its cost. A card with none of these keeps the line waits 2 min.
