1 · One card at two sizes, cut by its place

Every card is 5 : 7, at two sizes only: M (144 × 202) in the Orkmaster's column, the hand, the lanes and the discard, and L (404 × 566) opened. In the column the card lies (on the table, the Orkmaster's); in the hand it stands (yours to decide): the same card, turned a quarter when it comes to you. A place never changes the card's shape; it cuts it: the column's window edge cuts its left 45 px, the hand's window edge cuts its foot. What a size shows is its level of detail, and the crop lines are part of the card's template: what lies past a line is the card's secondary content, so a card slid or risen never shows an empty half, and no line of text is ever cut in two.

2 · Desktop

2.1 · The Orkmaster's column: every card lying, the hand's card on its side

Today a face-down card already lies sideways with its left 45 px under the window's edge (§236); a face-up card stands upright, 112 wide and as tall as its words (§249.1). Proposed: every card is the hand's card on its side, 202 × 144, face up as face down, and the column is 173 px wide (today 128). Its shown part (157 × 144) is its rune, its name in up to four lines of about 20 characters (about 80 in all, today about 42) and its foot; the tucked strip holds what is good to know and not needed to find it (the goal's colour, its tasks, how long it has been in the pile). Turning a card over is now just that: the same outline, the other side. Every pile is one height a card, so the column does not jump when a name grows. Hover a card to slide it out.

Hovered, the card rises by 74 px and shows its lower part: why it is asked (two lines, the leader's own reason), how long it waits and what it holds (tasks stopped behind it, or what it costs). 2.3 · A goal comes to your hand: the card turns upright

The card does not change its size on the way, only its lie: it slides out of its pile, turns a quarter on its way down and lands in the hand, cut at 128 like the others. Lying says the Orkmaster has it; standing says it is yours. Its face changes from the column's layout (rune, name, foot) to the hand's (the kind, who asks, the title) halfway through the turn. Under reduced motion it is in the hand at once.

2.4 · The opened card: whole, and it never grows

The lifted card is 404 × 566 today already. One rule is added: the card keeps its shape whatever it holds. A long question scrolls inside its body (the fade at its foot says there is more); the answer, the field and the buttons stay at its foot, always in reach. Same corner and title as the hand's card, so the card that rose is the card that opened.

3 · Phone

The phone's hand card is 80 × 112 already, 5 : 7, cut at 82 px. What changes there is what lies under the cut (today nothing), and that the crown's piles and the opened card take the same shapes as on the desktop. A touch has no hover: a card pressed rises and shows its lower part, a card tapped opens. The one place that leaves 5 : 7 is the opened card while the keyboard is up: 5 : 7 at a phone's width is 316 × 442, and a keyboard takes 250 px of a 700 px screen.

4 · What changes

Decisions changed: §249.1 (the column's face-up card 112 wide, its height its content's → the hand's card lying, 202 × 144, the name four lines at most); §236.1 and §236.3 (the face-down card 157 × 112 → 202 × 144; the column 128 px → 173 px, so the table is 45 px narrower and the hand's first lane as wide as a card); §249.2 (the hand's card about two thirds of 5 : 7 → 5 : 7 cut at the same height). §249.4's the height says where the work is becomes the crop says where the card lies.

What stays: the face-down cards' seal (§206.3, §241); the 45 px tuck; the hand's height and the table's; the lanes' cards and their windows (§249.3); the + card; the lifted card's 404 × 566; the narrow page's 80 × 112.

New in the card's template: the hand card's lower part (why, waits, holds), read from what the Board has: the question's own words, its time asked, the tasks whose step waits on it, its cost. Nothing drawn for its own sake: a card with none of these keeps the line waits 2 min.

The cost: the table 45 px narrower (at 1280 px a lane goes from about 174 to 165 px; a card still fits); each pile's front card 32 px taller, about 100 px down the column; a name over about 80 characters is cut on its fourth line (today the card grows a line). The phone's opened card leaves 5 : 7 while typing.
