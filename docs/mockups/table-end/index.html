1 · The table's end, always laid

The table's last column, under + Party, Stop all and Play, is empty today until a goal is done. Here both places lie there from the start, still and without colour: the portal's place is a ring of eight runes in the faint grey, and the discard's place is a card's outline in pixel dashes. Neither can be pressed, and neither moves. Colour and motion come only when there is something to do: a goal ready for main lights the runes one after another in the portal's violet, and then the portal opens inside the ring. The crops are the wide window's right end at 1 : 1 (1280 × 800).

2 · The + card: a seal of summoning

The new goal's card keeps its frame (the action colour's double line, the pixel corner brackets, the lean). The hatch gives way to a seal: a circle, a band of eight runes, and two squares crossed into an eight-pointed star round the +. The seal is drawn at about a quarter of the action colour's strength, so the + is still the one bright thing. On hover the runes light once, one after another, clockwise from the top, where today a band of light crosses the card. A goal is summoned at the +, and it leaves through the portal's ring, written in the same runes. Hover the cards.

3 · One alphabet

Twelve runes on a 5 × 7 grid (the signs' family: one-pixel strokes, square ends), Orkboard's own and not letters: they spell nothing and are read by no one. They are the one vocabulary of magic on the table, used by the + card's seal and the portal's ring. They could later go into the back's ring (backs/back.svg) as well, in the card frame's grey. Drawn at 4 px a cell here.
