1 · At rest: the heat on the parts

Tech's tab is open: System as it is built today, a container a column, its layers as rows, plants in the Shared column between the containers it serves, the one link an arrow over the lanes. What is new is the heat: at a card's top right, a hollow square for each kind of hint code saw on it (at most three), and the alert sign where the Architect said it hurts. screens is the hottest part: big, changed often, and with no test.

The heat is the hints, not a new number: each square is one kind of map_signals' tech hints on that part (hotspot, big, untested_churn, shared_churn, fan_in, unstable, cycle…), the hollow square spec 115 already uses for a hint. A leader's concern is the alert sign, as on Play. No count, no score, no colour of its own. Quiet at rest: which part a test checks is not drawn here. It matters only once something reaches it (2).

2 · A goal under the pointer in the hand: what it reaches

The pointer is on Snooze a plant, which waits to start. Its spec's Will be names two parts, routes and water: they get a solid edge in the goal's element (ice). What uses them gets a dashed edge, and what uses those a dotted one: api-client across the HTTP link, then screens and store. Everything else dims. Each reached card says at its top left whether a test checks it: the shield, or no test. The strip says it in one line.

Two steps and no further: a change reaches what uses it, and what uses that. A third step would light almost every part and say nothing. The link lights when the reach crosses it, so you see a change in api showing up in web. Read by code, no call: the parts named in Will be (decisions §156.4), uses and used_by from the snapshot, the tests from the gate's map (gate_config.gate_files, as untested_churn reads them).

3 · A wide reach: a goal through the shared part

The pointer moves to Reminders at the right hour (meadow). It changes plants, the part every container uses, so the reach goes over all three columns: it reaches seven parts, four of them with no test, and passes the hotspot screens. Nothing in the window forbids starting it. The person sees the cost before pressing Start.

What the shape gives: the person compares two goals by how far they spread before either starts, and Start stays the person's decision.

4 · The shared part lifted: the Architect's concern, and the way round

A press on plants while the reach is shown lifts its back: the Architect's concern of Coupling, and under it what code works out with no call: if the goal changed reminders alone, it would reach one part. The fix is the Architect's to propose, and the person makes it a goal or keeps the plan.

The way round is the same rule run on the parts the Fix leaves changed. It is shown only when the leader's Fix names the parts, and only on a part whose own reach is most of the whole.

5 · Under 760 px: the reach as a list

At the narrow width the tabs are heads and there is no room for columns of cards. The goal that was lifted (from the hand, a long press) shows its reach as three groups under the same edges: Changes, Uses them, Uses those. Each row is the part, its container, its heat and its test.

This is a mockup and is not wired into the app. Orkboard's parts are drawn in orkUI (the heads, the signs, the elements, the chips, the potions); Sproutly is spec 115's made-up project. Taken from the network game: heat that collects on a node, and a change that spreads to its neighbours. Not taken: a free graph of nodes (System stays the cards in columns, systemarch.js: never a diagram), an outbreak that spreads by itself or a timer, a score of risk or a percentage (numbers we do not have), the game's words in the interface (touches, reaches, no test say what happens), a reach for a goal whose spec does not yet say Will be (its strip line reads What it reaches is known once its spec says what will change). Motion: the edges come in nearest first (the parts it changes, then 120 ms later what uses them, then what uses those); nothing under prefers-reduced-motion.
