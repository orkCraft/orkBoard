# 168. The window played as a card game's table

Decisions §283, §284, §285 (the owner's of 9 November 2026). **Mockup:** `docs/mockups/table-template/` (its shots are the look;
its README says what it found). **The goal's page** `llm-wiki/general/pages/ideas/168-the-window-played-as-a-card-games-table.md`.
Talk is settled by the decisions themselves (every point is the owner's call, answered by its reference); this file is the spec.

**Builds:** a stage a Grunt on the simpler model, each in its own worktree, merged into the goal's branch
(`claude/ecstatic-turing-6rmro2`) and then into `main` on the critical tests and the stage's own (CLAUDE.md, alpha). A new
drawing is made in orkUI first (github.com/orkCraft/orkUI), released, then copied (`python -m orkboard.design.orkui <orkUI>`);
`design-system/orkui` is never edited here. The page's paths are under `orkboard/gui/static/js/`. **Waves**, one after the other,
the parts of a wave sharing no file run side by side: **1** A · **2** B · **3** C · **4** D · **5** E · **6** F · **7** G · **8** H ·
**9** I · **10** J. After each goal, its leader looks at its stages against their *Done when* (Design for the faces, Tech for the
engine), from the stage's diff, its tests and a picture at 1366 × 768.

**Every stage keeps**: the two sizes on the desktop, 144 × 202 and 56 × 78 (§283.1); nothing changing size by what is open
(§283.5); one place moving (§283.10); the words in `TERMS` (old spellings in `was`; `tests/test_gui_wording.py`); no colour,
font or corner of Orkboard's own (`tests/test_design_system.py`); `core/`, `realm/` with no face (`tests/test_architecture.py`);
no module past ~600 lines (split first); the narrow page (under 760 px) and the phone unchanged (§283's *Not now*).
