# orkUI / office / pennant

A swallowtail pennant on a bronze pole (Orkboard's decisions §269.4), in its party's colour. **`.ork-pennant`** (14 x 34) and **`.is-open`** (14 x 46, the open leader's: taller).

    pennant.json    the source: the grid (7 x 23 cells), the legend, the two states' rows (`G` finial, `P` pole, `F` flag, `h` light edge, `d` shade)
                    generated by tools/build_things.py into components.css (`python tools/build_things.py --check`)

    <span class="ork-pennant" data-party="ork" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-pennant is-open" data-party="knight" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- The flag is `--_party`: from the pennant's own `data-party` or the table's it stands in (`--ork-party-<race>`); the edge grey without one. `data-party` takes a race (ork, knight, elf, undead, goblin, gnome, orkmaster) or the party it leads (tech; game, product; design, art; release; run, liveops; market, marketing).
- 2 px a cell, `.small` 1 px. `box-shadow` pixels, never a blur. No words inside.
