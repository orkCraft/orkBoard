# orkUI / office / leather

The open backpack's frame (Orkboard's decisions §272.1): the table lies in stitched leather, two straps with gilt buckles over its top edge, **no rivets**. **`.ork-leather`** replaces the bronze frame of a box, and on `.ork-table[data-party]` the rivets too; the felt and its light stay.

    leather.json    the source: one strap of 10 x 20 cells (2 px a cell), its rectangles `[x, y, w, h, letter]`, the two places along the top edge (`at`: 22% and 78%)
                    generated by tools/build_things.py into components.css (`python tools/build_things.py --check`)

    <div class="ork-table ork-leather" data-party="tech">...</div>

- The frame is hard shadow rings in `--ork-leather-edge`, `--ork-leather`, `--ork-leather-hi`; the stitches a dashed outline in `--ork-stitch`; the straps flat layers. No blur, no gradient but flat fills.
