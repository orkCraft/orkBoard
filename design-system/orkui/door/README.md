# orkUI / office / door

The door: **Stop all's mark** (orkBoard's decisions §201.8, spec 123 part A). Orkboard's *Stop all* is a door at the dark row's end, **shut at rest, whatever runs**; pressed, it opens (the orks
go out) and says *All stopped*; it shuts when work starts again. Not *Exit*: it halts, it closes nothing. It is drawn at a sign's weight, in greys, with no planks (on a door shut by
default they would read *locked*), and **nothing swings**: shut and open are states.

    door.json       the source: the grid (14 x 20), the frame's rectangles `[x, y, width, height]` of cells, the interior, the knob's and the leaf's edge's cells
    frame.svg       the posts (2 cells wide) and the lintel (2 rows)
    interior.svg    what the frame encloses, x 2 to 11 and rows 2 to 19: the leaf (shut) or the opening (open)
                    generated by tools/build_door.py from door.json (`python tools/build_door.py --check`)

Each is a grid of unit squares, one `path` (`M{x} {y}h1v1h-1z` for every pixel, row by row from the top), `fill="#000"`, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`: no colour of its own, no metadata,
in the signs' format. The frame and the interior fill the 14 x 20 grid between them, and no cell is in both.

## Writing one

Plain HTML and CSS, no script and no inner markup:

    <span class="ork-door" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-door is-open" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-door small" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- **Shut**: the frame in `--ork-table-rim-hi`, the leaf (the whole interior) in `--ork-raised` and its knob (2 x 1 cells at x 9, row 10) in `--ork-ink-faint`.
- **`.is-open`**: the opening (the whole interior) in `--ork-ink-faint` at 0.7 and the leaf seen edge on, at its left (x 2 to 3), in `--ork-raised`; the frame as it was. The one light in a dark row.
- **Size**: the cell is 2 px by default (**28 x 40**), **`.small`** 1 px (**14 x 20**); whole pixels, no size between, the same on a phone.
- **`.is-lit`** (and `:hover`, `:focus-visible`) lights the frame in `--ork-edge`; the page sets it while the button the door is in is hovered or focused.
- **No words inside**: the caller adds the word (*Stop all*, in the danger colour while it can be pressed; *All stopped* once it has been) and the `title`; the door is `aria-hidden`. It is a
  box of its own (`inline-block`, `flex: none`, `vertical-align: bottom`).

The frame is the box's `::before` and the interior its `::after`, each a mask of the grid; the knob and the leaf's edge are background layers at the cells of `door.json` inside the interior's mask.
The drawing is that of Orkboard's mockup `docs/mockups/lens-heads/` (`doorSvg`). A shape is changed here, in the table of `tests/test_orkui.py` and in orkBoard's spec 123 together, never in a face.
