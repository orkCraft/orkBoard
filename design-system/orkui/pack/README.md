# orkUI / office / pack

The backpack (Orkboard's decisions §272.1): a leather backpack in pixels. **`.ork-pack`** shut (the flap folded over, buckled, stitched, a pocket) and **`.is-open`** (the flap thrown back, its buckle loose, a card in the dark mouth).

    pack.json    the source: the grid (14 x 18 cells), the legend (tokens), each state's rectangles `[x, y, w, h, letter]` of Orkboard's mockup docs/mockups/room-things/room.js, painted in order
                 generated by tools/build_things.py into components.css (`python tools/build_things.py --check`)

    <span class="ork-pack" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-pack is-open" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- 2 px a cell (**28 x 36**), `.small` 1 px (14 x 18). `box-shadow` pixels, never a blur; colours `--ork-leather*`, `--ork-stitch`, `--ork-gilt`, `--ork-room`, `--ork-ink`, `--ork-elem-meadow`.
- No words inside; the caller adds `aria-label` and `title`. Open, the table lies in its leather: see `leather/`.
