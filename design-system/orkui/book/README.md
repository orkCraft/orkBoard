# orkUI / office / book

The book (Orkboard's decisions §285.3): the history drawn as a book, shut and open as the backpack is. A tome lying flat, seen in three quarters, in pixels. **`.ork-book`** shut (the leather cover with a tooled border, bronze corners and a bronze clasp, the page block a light band, a ribbon bookmark hanging below the lower edge) and **`.is-open`** (the cover wider than the pages, two pages in a V over it, the ribbon across the gutter and out below).

    book.json    the source: the grid (24 x 25 cells), the legend (tokens), each state's rows, a letter a cell
                 generated by tools/build_things.py into components.css (`python tools/build_things.py --check`)

    <span class="ork-book" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-book is-open" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- 2 px a cell (**48 x 50**), `.small` 1 px (24 x 25). `box-shadow` pixels, never a blur; colours `--ork-leather*`, `--ork-bronze*`, `--ork-gilt`, the pages in `--ork-ink-read` and their lines in `--ork-ink-faint` (`--ork-paper` is the dark ground of a reading, so the light of a page is the reading ink).
- Its silhouette is wider than tall, so it is told from the upright backpack (`.ork-pack`) and the die's cube at 24 px.
- No words inside; the caller adds `aria-label` and `title`.
