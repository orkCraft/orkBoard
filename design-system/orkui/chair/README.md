# orkUI / office / chair

The chair: **a leader's seat at the table** (orkBoard's decisions §201.6, spec 123 part A). Orkboard sits each leader on one at the table's far edge, and **New party is the
empty chair**. It is drawn at a sign's weight, as the banner, the boards, the die and the potions are: two greys, no colour, never a picture of furniture, still at rest.
*Chair* is a picture and never a word (*seat* is the squad's).

    chair.json              the source: three chairs, each a grid (`w`, `h`) and, for its two tones, rectangles `[x, y, width, height]` of cells
    back-posts.svg, back-panel.svg      an occupied chair's back, 22 x 12
    big-posts.svg, big-panel.svg        the same for the open leader, 26 x 14
    empty-posts.svg, empty-panel.svg    the empty chair, whole, 18 x 18
                            generated by tools/build_chair.py from chair.json (`python tools/build_chair.py --check`)

Each is a grid of unit squares, one `path` (`M{x} {y}h1v1h-1z` for every pixel, row by row from the top), `fill="#000"`, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`: no colour of its
own, no metadata, in the signs' format. A chair is two masks, the **posts** (the lighter tone) and the **panel** (the darker); no cell is in both.

- **The back** (`.ork-chair`), 22 x 12 cells: two posts 2 cells wide the whole height (their knobs the top row), the top rail 2 rows high a cell under the top, the panel between the
  posts under the rail. The head stands over it, as one seated; the back is lower than the head. **`.big`** is 26 x 14, the same drawn wider and higher.
- **The empty chair** (`.ork-chair.is-empty`), 18 x 18 cells: two posts (x 2 to 3 and x 14 to 15, rows 0 to 9), the rail (rows 1 to 2) and the panel between them (rows 3 to 8), the
  seat the full width (rows 9 to 10) and the front legs (x 1 to 2 and x 15 to 16, rows 11 to 17).

## Writing one

Plain HTML and CSS, no script and no inner markup:

    <span class="ork-chair" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-chair big" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-chair is-empty" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-chair is-empty small" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-chair is-empty is-lit" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- **Size**: the cell is 2 px by default, so the back is **44 x 24**, `.big` **52 x 28** and the empty chair **36 x 36**; `.is-empty.small` is 1 px a cell (**18 x 18**). Whole pixels,
  no size between, the same on a phone. The box is the chair's own (`inline-block`, `flex: none`, `vertical-align: bottom`); the page stands it on the table's rim and puts the head over it.
- **Colour**: an occupied chair's posts and rail are `--ork-table-rim-hi` and its panel `--ork-table-rim`. The empty chair is fainter, its posts `--ork-frame` and its panel
  `--ork-raised`. **`.is-lit`** (the empty chair's, on the element itself; **`:hover`** and **`:focus-visible`** too) takes it to `--ork-edge` and `--ork-table-rim`. No colour beyond these tokens.
- **Motion**: lit, the empty chair is **pulled out from its foot** (up 2 px and turned -4 degrees: `transform: translateY(-2px) rotate(-4deg)`, `transform-origin: 50% 100%`) on `--ork-motion-base` and
  the spring, **only inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`**. Under reduced motion it is lit (the colours change) and does not move. An occupied chair never moves.
- **No words inside**: the caller says what it is (the label of the button the empty chair sits in is *New party*), and the chair is `aria-hidden`. When the page lights an empty chair by a press on its
  button, it puts `is-lit` on the chair, or lets the chair's own `:hover` do it.

The two tones are the potion's two layers: the box's `::before` draws the posts (the rail, the seat and the legs), its `::after` the panel, each a mask of its grid. The drawing is that of
Orkboard's mockup `docs/mockups/lens-heads/` (`chairSvg`). A shape is changed here, in the table of `tests/test_orkui.py` and in orkBoard's spec 123 together, never in a face.
