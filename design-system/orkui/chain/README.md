# orkUI / office / chain

The lamp's chain (Orkboard's decisions §271, in place of the door): bronze links hung from the window's top edge and a bead to pull. **`.ork-chain`** at rest, **`.is-pulled`** four rows longer with the bead lower.

    chain.json    the source: the grid (6 x 29 cells), the legend (tokens), the two states' rows
                  generated by tools/build_things.py into components.css (`python tools/build_things.py --check`)

    <span class="ork-chain" aria-hidden="true"></span>
    <span class="ork-chain is-pulled" aria-hidden="true"></span>

- 2 px a cell (**12 x 58**), `.small` 1 px (6 x 29); whole pixels. Drawn in `box-shadow` pixels (two lengths a cell, never a blur) in `--ork-bronze-hi`, `--ork-bronze`, `--ork-bronze-lo`.
- **`.is-tugging`**: the beat of a pull, once, only when motion is allowed (`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`): the chain drops by `--ork-motion-rise` and is back, on `--ork-motion-base`, in whole steps. The page adds it with `.is-pulled` and takes it off at the animationend `ork-chain-tug`; under reduced motion the state is at once.
- No words inside: the caller says *Here* or *Away* beside it and in `aria-label` and `title`.
- It stands where `door/` stood; `door/` stays in this release, its users move later.
